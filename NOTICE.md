# Notices

Zenith is MIT-licensed software. The night sky it draws is older than that.

## Hipparcos

Star positions, magnitudes, and B−V colours in `public/data/sky.json` are
packed from the ESA Hipparcos Catalogue (Perryman et al. 1997). Hipparcos is a
scientific catalogue published by the European Space Agency. This project is
not affiliated with ESA.

Please retain this acknowledgment in downstream distributions of the packed
catalogue.

## IAU constellations

The 88 figures and three-letter identifiers follow the International
Astronomical Union. Serpens is one IAU constellation drawn as two figures
(Caput and Cauda) that share the identifier `Ser`.

## Lineage

Constellation line paths and common-name tables were adapted from public
planetarium sources in the d3-celestial family (Olaf Frohn, BSD). The packed
JSON in this repository is a derived, magnitude-limited extract (Hipparcos
stars to mag 6), not a verbatim copy of any upstream file.
